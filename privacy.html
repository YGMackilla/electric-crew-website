Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Electric Crew LGBTQ+ ('we', 'us', or 'our') operates the website electriccrewvr.xyz and the Electric Crew Events Discord bot. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our services.

2. Information We Collect

DJ bookings: when you claim a DJ slot we store the name you enter, an optional Discord handle, and the time you claimed it. Arcade: if you save a score we store the name or handle you type, your score and run length. Contact: if the contact form is enabled, we receive the name, reply address and message you send. This website does not use advertising trackers, and we do not ask you to create an account.

3. Discord Bot

The Electric Crew Events bot creates and updates events and DJ slots in our Discord server and our database. It does not read messages, access private channels, or collect personal information about individual Discord users.

4. How We Use Information

Booking details are shown publicly on the event lineup so the community knows who is playing. Arcade names and scores are shown on the public crew board. Contact messages are used only to reply to you. We do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Storage

Events, DJ bookings and arcade scores are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Your browser also keeps a few small preferences on your own device, such as light or dark mode, your best arcade score and the name you last used. You can clear these any time in your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website links to Discord, VRChat, Instagram and The Trevor Project, and loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you use those services you are subject to their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

7. Cookies

We do not set tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

Electric Crew is an 18+ community and our services are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to remove a booking or arcade score, or stop using the services at any time. Because we collect very little, most requests are quick to resolve.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will tell the community about significant changes in our Discord server.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out through our Discord server at discord.gg/uubT9dKxT.
